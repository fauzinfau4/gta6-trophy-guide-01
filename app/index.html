About this ledger

Leonida Ledger is an independent, offline fan guide. Trophy names, descriptions and grades reproduce the list supplied with this competition. This app is not verified by Rockstar Games or PlayStation.

How to read the notes

Named mission and count details come from the cited trophy guide sources. When a precise route is not corroborated, the note says so and sticks to what the trophy description itself establishes. Missable flags are conservative: confirm before passing a story point of no return.
